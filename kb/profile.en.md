# Seung Ho Bae — Profile

## Current role
Seung Ho Bae is an AI Platform Engineer (Senior Associate) in the Customer Contact Innovation Department at KB Kookmin Bank. He joined in December 2022 and designs and operates customer-facing AI services with Python, Kubernetes, and AWS.

## Experience
- [2026.05–present] Developing new features for and operating KB's video consultation system, KB's flagship non-face-to-face product subscription system. Customers join remotely, go through verification and a product explanation, then subscribe to products such as trust products and new deposit accounts, or use financial services such as account password changes. His work includes the verification flow that combines ID authenticity checks with face matching.
- [2026.04–2026.05] Generative AI customer chatbot at KB. In the project to turn a rule-based chatbot into a generative one, he developed the agent, built topic-based knowledge bases by embedding product descriptions and terms, and evaluated the agent's performance. He did not build the whole chatbot.
- [2025.06–2026.03] Built an internal employee-efficiency platform, an AI training-data management platform that reduces manual work and duplicated management across multiple chatbots. It was an in-house project within the group, built with one junior developer, and he covered most of the development: infrastructure, cloud, web server, AI models, and analysis features.
- [2022.12–2024.09] Developed and operated the AI Financial Assistant, a photorealistic-avatar service, at the Financial AI Center. He managed EKS, optimized cloud costs with Lambda scheduling and HPA, and built DevOps and CI/CD pipelines.
- [2022.07–2022.08] Research intern at ETRI's Medical IT Convergence Lab, developing a deep learning model that estimates blood pressure from PPG and ECG biosignals.
- [2022.03–2022.06] Intern at Qisens AI, developing and optimizing an aerial-image segmentation model and applying a shadow-removal GAN.
