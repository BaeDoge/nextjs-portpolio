## Background

Details from a bug investigation or a newly learned technology are easy to forget over time. A solution found through search may also stop working when the original environment or library version changes.

I wanted a technical notebook that records more than the final fix. It should explain how an issue was reproduced, which causes were investigated, and what change actually resolved it. The same site also covers algorithm exercises, paper reviews, and open-source analysis.

Live site: [Baejoon — 함께 공부합시다](https://baejoon.vercel.app)

The name takes inspiration from Baekjoon, the well-known algorithm practice platform in Korea. The idea is similar: solve a problem, verify the approach, and keep a useful record of the solution.

## Implementation

<TechStack items={["Next.js", "React", "MDX", "Markdown", "Vercel"]} />

Articles are stored as MDX files grouped by category rather than being hard-coded into page components. This separates content from presentation and makes it possible to add or revise articles without changing the application structure.

~~~text
content/
  algorithm/
  troubleshooting/
  paper/
  opensource/

Next.js App Router
        │
        ▼
MDX metadata + article content
        │
        ├── Category / Article pages
        ├── Search / Related posts
        └── Article metadata / SEO
~~~

## Editorial Approach

For paper reviews and open-source articles, I start with the original paper or repository and run relevant examples where practical. Troubleshooting articles include the environment and reproduction context, rather than only listing the command that fixed the issue.

AI tools help me explore topics, organise source material, draft explanations, and review code. I do not treat generated explanations as verified facts: I check original references and practical results before publishing.

## Maintenance

As the article collection grows, it becomes important to revisit old explanations, update version-specific information, and repair outdated links. I want Baejoon to remain useful as a technical reference, not simply a stream of newly published posts.

## Takeaways

Baejoon is less about publishing as often as possible and more about keeping development knowledge reusable. Writing down a problem, verifying the explanation, and preserving a working example are all part of the learning process.
