## About the Competition

The KB Future Finance AI Challenge was an AI competition hosted by KB Kookmin Bank.
The task was to develop an AI-based, customer-friendly service for customers who do their banking without face-to-face contact
through KB's modern ATM, the STM (Smart Teller Machine).

## Team

The team had three members, and I was the team leader and in charge of AI model development.

## Idea and Development

Customers who use an STM handle their banking alone, without a teller.
Older customers and those less familiar with digital tools often have trouble using an STM.

To solve this, we collected initial video data of the customer who came to use the STM
and served a landing page customized by age group,
building the system so it could give guidance at the customer's level.

We also applied **Peeking Detection** so that other people cannot see the screen during STM transactions,
and implemented a feature that assists identity verification by matching the ID card image against the customer in front of the screen.

## Tech Stack

`Python` `Computer Vision` `MediaPipe` `Natural Language Processing` `Financial Service Design`

## Award

**KB Future Finance AI Challenge Excellence Award** (2021, KB Kookmin Bank)

## What It Led To

Having directly proposed a direction for KB Kookmin Bank's AI services in this competition
led to joining KB Kookmin Bank and developing the AI Financial Assistant.
