## About the Service

The AI Financial Assistant is a customer-facing service in which a photorealistic AI avatar talks directly with customers
and handles financial tasks as well as everyday conversation about weather, hobbies, and more.

It was first unveiled in March 2021 at the AI Experience Zone in the new building of KB Kookmin Bank in Yeouido,
and it started from about 100 conversation scenarios and kept being improved.

## My Role

- **Cloud operations and optimization**

I was in charge of orchestrating the NLP, Vision, and Hub servers built on AWS.

After running it for the first few months,
I found that keeping the servers at full capacity around the clock, even at hours with almost no customer traffic, was costing an enormous amount.

I applied Lambda scheduling to automatically scale pods down outside business hours,
and configured HPA (Horizontal Pod Autoscaler) to scale up automatically when traffic spikes.

For the GPU servers in particular, which cost a lot per hour, I requested a new Savings Plan,
which cut operating costs well below the existing on-demand instances.

- **DevOps and CI/CD setup**

I built a CI/CD pipeline based on Jira and Jenkins inside the internal network.

For each new release, a ticket was issued through Jira, a new Docker image was built, and it was deployed to EKS with a rolling update.
There was no downtime during deployment, so version management could be done safely.

- **Training data management and monitoring**

I analyzed the conversation logs of the AI Financial Assistant and managed the appropriate training queries to improve answer quality.

## Tech Stack

`Kubernetes` `AWS EKS` `Lambda` `HPA` `Docker` `CI/CD` `Python`

## Results

At KB Kookmin Bank, I built and operated a photorealistic-avatar financial assistant that had not existed before, as my first project there.

I gave many presentations at corporate AI forums and conferences, and it was especially expected to be a service that middle-aged and older customers could use comfortably.


## Lesson & Learned

I learned that AI services built at a company incur far more server cost than you would imagine. (The monthly bill was more than my salary.)

Cost matters, of course, but this project showed me that the most important goal is building a service that improves the customer experience.
