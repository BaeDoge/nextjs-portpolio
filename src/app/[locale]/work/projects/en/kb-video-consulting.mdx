## About the Service

The KB Video Consultation System is KB Kookmin Bank's flagship non-face-to-face product subscription system.
Customers join a video consultation from KB Star Banking and other channels without visiting a branch. After identity verification and a product explanation, they can subscribe to products and use financial services in one session.

```text
Customer joins remotely
    ↓
Identity verification (ID authenticity check + face matching)
    ↓
Product explanation by a specialist
    ↓
Product subscription / financial services
```
