## ETRI Research

In the summer of 2022 I did a two-month internship at the Medical IT Convergence Research Lab of the ETRI Daegyeong Research Center.

My task was to develop a **deep learning model that estimates blood pressure from PPG signals**.
The goal was to measure blood pressure with just a finger sensor and no cuff (the blood pressure band),
but the sensor data itself has low precision and high variability, so it was not an easy project.

For developing the model, I used the MIMIC-III medical dataset.

## 3-Branch Functional Model

Most earlier studies fed the PPG waveform into an LSTM sequentially.
But I realized there are more time-domain features that can be extracted from PPG.

<Grid fillWidth columns="2" gap="16" marginBottom="16" marginTop="8">
  <Column border="neutral-alpha-medium" padding="16" radius="m" gap="8">
    <Text variant="label-strong-s">Waveform shape (Morphology)</Text>
    <Text variant="body-default-s" onBackground="neutral-weak">Morphological properties of the waveform itself, such as systolic peak height, diastolic trough position, and the shape of the dicrotic notch</Text>
  </Column>
  <Column border="neutral-alpha-medium" padding="16" radius="m" gap="8">
    <Text variant="label-strong-s">Time-domain features</Text>
    <Text variant="body-default-s" onBackground="neutral-weak">Time-based statistics such as BPM, inter-beat interval (IBI), rise time, and fall time</Text>
  </Column>
</Grid>

When I fed both into a single input, the model could not process the two kinds of features independently and performance did not change,
so I split it into three branches.

```text
[PPG waveform]       → Branch 1 (General CNN-LSTM) ─┐
[PPG waveform]       → Branch 2 (Wide CNN-LSTM)    ─┼→ Dense → SBP prediction
[Time features]      → Branch 3 (Time-domain DNN)  ─┘
```

Branches 1 and 2 use different window sizes to capture the local and global characteristics of the sensor data,
and Branch 3 takes time-domain data extracted with the Heartpy library.

The improvement was largest when the time-domain branch was added.
It means blood-pressure-related information that the waveform shape alone misses is contained in the time domain.

## Lesson & Learned

"Understanding the data comes before model architecture." I think this was the first project where I properly felt how true that is.
I came up with the branch structure only by taking the PPG signal apart myself.

I worked at the institute for just two months, but I gained experience building a model that takes time-series data as input,
and to leave something behind from what I learned, I wrote a short paper and presented it at an academic conference.
