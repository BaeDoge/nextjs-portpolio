## About the Competition

The SK AI Challenge for Our Society was an AI competition hosted by SK Inc.
The task was to use camera footage from autonomous vehicles
to build a model that detects road elements such as lanes, traffic lights, and pedestrians.

I entered the contest with my favorite dog "Doge" (back then it was not the name of a cryptocurrency!) plus vision.

## Core Approach: Semi-Supervised Learning

The central challenge of this contest was the **shortage of labeled data**.
Labeled data was limited, while unlabeled data was abundant.

Training with fully supervised learning alone would limit performance because of the lack of data.
To address this, I built a model based on **semi-supervised learning**.

I generated pseudo labels from the unlabeled data
and used only high-confidence predictions for training,
which effectively expanded the labeled data.

## Tech Stack

`Python` `PyTorch` `YOLO` `Semi-Supervised Learning` `Pseudo Labeling` `OpenCV`

## Results

**Excellence Award** (SK AI Challenge for Our Society, 2021)

## Lesson & Learned

I figured that real autonomous driving data would be overwhelmingly unlabeled.

It was a project that taught me that unsupervised and semi-supervised methods, such as semi-supervised and self-supervised learning,
are essential for solving real-world problems.

I entered this one alone while preparing for other competitions.
The two months of the project were very hard, but I was able to leave a result behind, so I think it was a very rewarding experience.
