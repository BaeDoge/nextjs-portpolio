Today I'll write up my own criteria for reading tech news.

## Background

I checked GeekNews and Hacker News every day, but little of it stayed with me for the time I spent.

## Criteria I applied

**1. Will I use it this quarter?**

If I have no plan to use a technology soon, I check only the title and move on.

**2. Are the benchmark conditions stated?**

I skip performance comparisons that don't state what was compared, the dataset, or the hardware.

**3. Failure stories first**

Failure stories are more reproducible than success stories, so I read them first.

## Changing how I apply them

I changed from checking every day to a 30-minute session on weekends. I record what I read in one line, and if I can't summarize something, I treat it as a sign I didn't understand it well enough.

## Conclusion

Setting clear filtering criteria and checking periodically was more efficient than following all the news in real time.
