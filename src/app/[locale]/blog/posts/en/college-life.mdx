## Overview

It feels like I was heading off to a university MT (membership trip) just the other day, yet it has already been over three years since I graduated.

I didn't get to play around as much as others did, but looking back, I think I spent my university years fairly well, so I decided to write it down.

## Returning to School After Military Service

I was discharged in October 2019 and went back to school. By then my classmates were two years ahead of me, preparing for their senior year.
They had worked on lots of projects in their third and fourth years and were getting ready for job hunting without any trouble.

I had played far too much in my first year and didn't really know anything, so I felt envious and anxious at the same time.

So in early 2020 I joined the MCL (Mobile Communication Lab) research lab,
and that was when I first started studying AI.

## 2021: The Year I Got Hooked on Competitions

At the suggestion of a PhD researcher in the lab I entered my first competition and was eliminated,
but strangely I enjoyed it. I enjoyed the process of turning an idea into a real result.
From then on I studied hard alongside my coursework and applied to a great many competitions.

In one year I entered more than 30 times and got results in more than 10.
I was eliminated more often than not, but each time I wrote down what had fallen short
and submitted a more polished piece of work to the next competition.

At the same time, I served as deputy leader of the **Youth Policy Participation Group** organized by Gyeongsan City, in both its 1st and 2nd terms,
and shared my own opinions on the city's youth programs.
I also served as education officer of **IT's**, an academic club on campus, where I taught coding to younger students after classes.

[IT's news coverage (look closely and you'll find me in it)](https://www.etnews.com/20220114000054)

## 2022: Two Internships and a Job

When I became a fourth-year student and it was time to prepare for employment,
I did internships at Qisens in March and ETRI in June while also working on my graduation project.

At Qisens I built an aerial image segmentation model.
It felt like a first real company, different from the lab, and I got to see actual work alongside colleagues.
I think I came to feel how different experimental, proof-of-concept code is from real service code.

The day after the Qisens internship ended, I started a summer internship at **ETRI**.
I built a model that estimates blood pressure from PPG signals and did research together with the skilled PhD researchers there.
Toward the end of the two-month internship I wrote up what I had learned and presented it myself as a paper at the Korean Institute of Communications and Information Sciences.

I spent the second half of the year focused on job hunting,
and in December I was accepted by **KB Kookmin Bank**, where I work now.
After about three weeks of rest I moved to Seoul right away, and in February 2023 I graduated on a full scholarship.

## Looking Back

- What went well

> Taking on many challenges and gaining a lot of experience.

> Making full use of my time with no idle stretches.

- What I regret

> Barely using GitHub, so I didn't organize my work properly.

> Never trying programs like studying abroad or credit exchange.

> Not building more of the memories and friendships that are only possible as a university student.

- What university gave me

> A mindset of trying again even after failing.

> The ability to produce results with the best use of time and cost.
