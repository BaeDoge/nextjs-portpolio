Today I'll write up a cloud cost optimization case from the KB Kookmin Bank AI Financial Assistant service.

## Situation

Traffic was concentrated during business hours (weekdays, 9 AM to 6 PM) and almost nonexistent at night and on weekends. Keeping pods at the same spec around the clock meant paying the same cost even during idle hours.

## Fix 1: Lambda Scheduling

I used AWS Lambda to adjust the number of pods by time of day.

```python
def scale_eks_deployment(replicas: int, deployment: str):
    k8s_client.patch_namespaced_deployment_scale(
        name=deployment,
        namespace="production",
        body={"spec": {"replicas": replicas}}
    )
```

## Fix 2: HPA Configuration

To handle unpredictable traffic increases, I also configured HPA.

```yaml
apiVersion: autoscaling/v2
kind: HorizontalPodAutoscaler
spec:
  minReplicas: 2
  maxReplicas: 10
  metrics:
  - type: Resource
    resource:
      name: cpu
      target:
        type: Utilization
        averageUtilization: 70
```

I applied a structure where regular patterns are handled by Lambda scheduling and sudden traffic by HPA. Details such as thresholds and wait times were tuned against real traffic data.
